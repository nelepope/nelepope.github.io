What is a web copy audit?

A structured, objective review of your current website copy (the words on your website). It gives you an idea of what is working and what isn't, and what you can do to improve it.

What you get

  • A review of three pages of web copy or equivalent
  • A detailed list of any issues and what to do about them
  • A video call to walk through my suggestions
  • Ideas for new website copy and SEO content

all for £180

Things I will look at

  • Is your core message presented clearly?
  • Does your copy sound like you, and reflect what your business stands for?
  • Is it written for the right audience?
  • Is it easy to read and scan on a screen?
  • Are you using SEO keywords, headings and meta descriptions effectively?
  • Is the tone consistent?
  • Are your calls to action clear and easy to find?
