Goldilocks:
copy foundation

Brand story, founder story, messaging pillars, customer profiles and tone of voice for a speciality coffee brand with precisely measured caffeine.

By Penelope Saint-George Brand strategy sample

Brand story

Goldilocks offers speciality coffee with precisely calibrated amounts of caffeine. This way, you can enjoy a controlled boost all day without worrying about jitters, poor sleep, and the other dreaded symptoms of over-caffeination. We use speciality-grade beans and the Swiss Water Process for a delicious, chemical-free brew that gives you just the right amount of lift when you want it.

Uncompromising coffee.

Simon's story

I grew up in Melbourne, Australia, one of the great coffee cities in the world. I loved the ritual, the taste, the smell of coffee. I loved having the excuse to sit somewhere and chat.

Then, during business school, I drank five cups a day. I'd be wired at night, and shattered in the morning. Then I'd need another cup of coffee to get going again.

When I finally, and reluctantly, quit caffeine, my sleep immediately got better. With this came a broader shift, away from the "down the coffee and grind" mindset and towards something more intentional, paying attention to my body, slowing down, and enjoying life.

I tried decaf, but it tasted cheap and flavourless. It's also made using chemical solvents. I tried drinking tea, cacao, or mushrooms, but they never hit the spot that coffee did.

Then two things changed:

One - I read about the Swiss Water Process, a decaffeination process using water and activated charcoal. It removes 99.9% of the caffeine content without chemicals and without killing the flavour.

Two - During a heavy cold, I took a flu tablet with 25mg of caffeine in it. It gave me lift without jitters.

I started mixing regular and decaf beans at precise ratios. I found my sweet spot around 30-40mg per cup. This precise amount of caffeine gave me the boost I wanted without losing sleep at night. I could finally enjoy coffee again!

I discovered a huge number of people are quietly managing caffeine sensitivity. Everyone had a different hacky workaround, but nobody had a real solution.

So I created Goldilocks. Coffee in 5 caffeine levels: 0 / 25 / 50 / 75 / 100.

Your number is yours. It might change by the time of day, the season, or just how you're feeling. That's the whole point. Coffee that fits your life, not the other way around.

The problem

Drinking coffee results in:

  • Restlessness, jitteriness
  • Racing / irregular heartbeat
  • Anxiety
  • Difficulty sleeping
  • Headaches
  • Dizziness
  • Muscle tremors
  • Gastrointestinal issues (nausea, upset stomach)

Problems giving up coffee include:

  • Withdrawal symptoms
  • Having to be the person who "doesn't drink coffee"
  • Decaf is usually made with chemicals and isn't as good
  • Having to order decaf and being a bit embarrassed about that
  • Missing out on the ritual
  • Missing out on the boost
  • Missing out on the social aspect of coffee

The solution

Goldilocks gives you the ability to enjoy high-quality coffee at any time of day, without sacrifice. It gives you the ability to calibrate the precise amount of caffeine intake to suit you.

Values

Quality · Craft · Precision · Autonomy/Freedom · Intentionality · Honesty · Simplicity · Optimisation

Key messaging pillars

Precision

The differentiator. What differentiates other decaf/coffee companies from Goldilocks: coffee offered with 5 calibrated caffeine levels using a specific namable process. Precision is a truth and a brand value. Precision gives the customer command over their day and how they feel.

Craft

For credibility. Goldilocks is about specialty-grade coffee first, then the caffeine second. (Could talk about you wanting to drink either good decaffeinated coffee or no coffee.) Goldilocks combines the art of good coffee with the science and precision of decaffeination via the Swiss Water Process. Goldilocks is for people who want to enjoy the simple pleasures of life without sacrifice.

Intention

The values. Paying attention, less is more, caring about the little things that matter, enjoying the simple pleasures of life, and living life well. These values connect to both calibrator and connoisseur. The values make Goldilocks a brand rather than just a series of products. This gives you something to talk about beyond coffee. Use your story and personal experiences to talk about the values.

Ritual

People want to buy feelings, not products. Goldilocks offers coffee for the experience, not just the function. The smell, the taste, the social excuse, the moment of pause. Goldilocks is about enjoying this without sacrifice. Without this pillar, Goldilocks risks feeling a bit utilitarian. Ritual is what makes someone feel something when they read the copy.

The customer

The calibrator

An optimiser. Mindful of performance. Biohacking adjacent.

  • Wears an Oura ring / fitbit / tracks HRV
  • May know their Zoe score
  • Meditates
  • Already thinks about what they put into their body, and may avoid certain foods like dairy
  • Sees five specific caffeine levels as a tool, not a gimmick
  • Drinks coffee after the gym or as a focus aid

Values: self-knowledge, precision, control, efficiency, health, well-being

Probably has a senior role at work. Has enough autonomy over his day to think carefully about how he structures it. The morning routine matters a lot because it's the one part of the day that's his before everything else starts demanding things from him.

Exercises consistently. Runs casually - maybe runs home from work/does park run at the weekend. Exercise is about mental clarity as well as fitness. Has probably done a half marathon.

Has done a Zoe programme or seriously considered it. Knows his resting heart rate. Has experimented with intermittent fasting and still does a loose version of it. Doesn't drink much alcohol anymore, not for any dramatic reason; it just stopped making sense with how he wants to feel.

Coffee is part of his morning ritual and a focus tool later in the day. He's already aware that caffeine affects his sleep - not a new discovery for him. What he hasn't had is a precise, reliable way to manage it. He's probably tried cutting back, going full decaf, and found neither quite right. The five caffeine levels are immediately legible to him as a tool. He gets it straight away.

What he responds to: exact numbers, specificity, anything that treats caffeine as a variable worth optimizing. References to sleep quality, HRV, and recovery. The Swiss Water Process matters to him because of the lack of chemicals (he'll have read "Ultraprocessed People" by Chris van Tulleken).

What puts him off: vague claims, anything that can't be verified, and over-promising. He's allergic to marketing language. If the copy feels like it's trying too hard, he'll disengage.

His moment of purchase: probably not impulsive. He reads about it, sits with it, and maybe looks up the Swiss Water Process independently. He just needed enough credible information to make the buying decision feel sound. He becomes a loyal customer because once something works for him, it goes into the system and stays there.

Specialty coffee person. Loves talking about beans and roasting. Has all the coffee kit.

  • Has a selection of specialty coffees at home / seeks out specialty coffee shops
  • Loves to talk about coffee processes
  • Goldilocks permits them to be interested in, and care about, decaf

Strategy

Focus on the precision side of things / the ability to control caffeine intake, because that is what makes Goldilocks unique. There is less competition this way than with the decaf coffee market.

Key messages by audience

The calibrator

Principle: This person thinks in systems. They track sleep, monitor what they eat, and are already aware that caffeine affects their performance. They don't need to be convinced that caffeine has drawbacks. They DO need to know that Goldilocks gives them genuine control over exactly how much caffeine they consume and how it can improve their life. Lead with precision, use exact numbers, and be specific.

Most people have no idea how much caffeine is in their morning cup. Goldilocks does. Five levels, from 0 to 100mg, using specialty-grade beans and the Swiss Water Process. Each caffeine level is precisely measured so you can calibrate your intake to the time of day, your training, and your sleep. Find your optimal dose. Adjust when you need to. Experience better sleep and sharper mornings.Example

The connoisseur

Principle: This person already cares deeply about coffee quality. Their barrier isn't caffeine sensitivity but rather the assumption that decaf means bad coffee. Talk about craft and flavour. Use the Swiss Water Process as a credibility signal, make it interesting. Reframe decaf as a deliberate, knowledgeable choice rather than a compromise. Let them know they don't have to sacrifice anything in order to enjoy high-quality coffee.

Goldilocks uses the Swiss Water Process (no chemicals, just water) applied to specialty-grade beans sourced and roasted for flavour first. The caffeine is optional. The quality isn't.Example
